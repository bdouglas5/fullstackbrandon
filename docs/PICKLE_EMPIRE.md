# Pickle Empire: career simulation

## The loop

A new career starts on foot, with zero money, no gadgets, and no vehicles. Supplier stock gives Brandon a way to earn his first delivery fees. The deterministic controller serves available orders, obtains required handling tools, acquires transport in order, hires a crew, fills the workshop, improves gadgets, opens outposts, and builds a retirement reserve. The default private Jev AI controller selects from the same legal action set, with an explicitly labeled deterministic fallback.

The default fleet ladder is bicycle → van → sailboat → helicopter → jetpack → teleporter. Each generation is used before the next automatic purchase. The sailing boat unlocks regular island business. Helicopters, jetpacks, and portals change travel time, cargo limits, weather constraints, and battery use. Stock in excess of aircraft capacity stays at the departure pad and is restored on return; it never disappears. Employees travel at 68% of Brandon’s speed and earn a one-coin commission per completed delivery.

Harvests replenish supply, while generated cases are added to the accounting ledger. Inventory is conserved across warehouses, founder cargo, temporarily stowed cargo, courier cargo, outposts, deliveries, waste, and recorded losses. Money reconciles earnings, purchases, wages, and explicitly recorded visitor grants.

## Customers and reputation

Twelve identifiable owners operate on the home islands, Juniper Quay, Dill Ridge, Copperport, Festival Key, Sunset Bay, and Reef Island. Their order records include identity, location, product, patience, handling requirement, arrival tick, and assigned courier. Delivery only fulfills an assigned order when actual cargo reaches the destination.

After every three completed orders, that customer posts a review. Recent on-time counts and average wait determine the star rating and the authored review text. The UI shows on-time counts and a plain-language timing summary; exact waits remain recorded in the simulation. The aggregate rating covers all reviews; the latest sixty review cards remain visible. These are simulated customers making rule-based judgments, not real-world testimonials or invented model reasoning.

## Visitor control

Visitors can queue an affordable or future purchase, choose a transport, hire an employee, guide service priorities, or introduce bounded disruptions. A queued investment remains pending while the business earns the required resources. Road and sea journeys finish at safe boundaries. Visitor storms eventually return to the natural weather cycle so the career can recover without another click.

The scene has road routing, island-safe water waypoints, aircraft launch/cruise/landing stages, on-foot handoffs, jointed walking and cycling, product models, night lighting, rain, mist, stars, and fireflies. Brandon, customers, employees and seated riders now share the same small world scale. Follow controls select the founder or a courier.

## Completion

Retirement requires all six purchased vehicles, actual use of every mode, ten Pro gadgets, three hired employees, and the visible 2,500-coin reserve. Active journeys finish before the retirement card appears. A new story can start from zero; replay and the recorded decisions remain available for inspection.

## Verification boundaries

Deterministic whole-career tests establish progression and conservation; a production HTTP/SSE career verifies the real server path. Browser flows exercise the entry point, interventions, persistence, illustrated catalogs, reviews, employee follow, night rendering, every transport, and retirement layering. Later-stage render tests use snapshots generated by actual unattended engine runs in the isolated test database. They are labeled fixtures, not evidence that the browser itself waited through every career tick.

Public hosting and real-device GPU performance are separate from local correctness. See `VERIFICATION.md` for the exact current evidence.
