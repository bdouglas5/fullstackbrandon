# Achievements

The Achievements panel is an icon tree with a movable viewport. Drag with a mouse or touch to pan. Mouse-wheel zoom preserves the world point under the cursor; arrow keys, zoom buttons, recenter, and Fit whole tree are also available. Zoom ranges from the scale needed to fit the entire map to 3×. The map fills all remaining panel height. Only the nearby portion of the map is rendered. Small icon shortcuts move between the seven branches; hovering or selecting a trophy shows its requirement and progress. Locked trophies are gray, undiscovered trophies use question marks, and collected trophies carry a gold check. The compact Collected list jumps directly to earned trophies. There are no Full tree / Up next / Earned filters or retirement checklist in this panel.

`src/lib/achievement-map.js` arranges prerequisite families vertically with straight branching connections, places trophies with multiple prerequisites below their parent families, and packs the seven branches into two columns for a compact overview. Branch hubs and the journey marker organize navigation; they do not add engine unlock conditions. `Achievements.jsx` handles pointer capture after a drag threshold, suppresses selection after dragging, bounds the camera, virtualizes nearby icons, and supports keyboard navigation and reduced motion.

`shared/achievements.js` defines stable IDs, prerequisites, seeded challenges, engine predicates, discovery states, and deterministic map layout. The engine evaluates only the current run's achievements. A past trophy reveals its following paths, but cannot satisfy another run's prerequisites or live retirement requirements. Retirement uses the same live predicates as the engine. Achievements grant no money or gameplay bonuses.

The SQLite `achievement_collections` table keeps each session's first unlock records, including run ID, seed, simulation minute, and legacy recognition. Save and load merge current trophies into that collection; load also imports recorded trophies from older runs. New businesses start with no current-run unlocks and retain the collection. Replay frames retain the collection at the time they were saved. Collection ownership follows the existing browser session and its seven-day inactivity retention; clearing the session cookie does not transfer trophies to a new session.

The New business link advances the seed by one (wrapping at 100000). Demand intervals vary deterministically by seed. Three mutually exclusive 50-delivery trophies correspond to the three demand patterns: Fast lane (e.g. 42), Finding balance (43), and Patient builder (44). One run can earn at most 78 of 80 trophies; all 80 are reachable across runs. Late volume milestones can require a continued chapter, and island outposts can be pursued through normal business guidance.

Live banners still queue current-run unlocks for six seconds, pause on hover/focus, defer during dialogs, and suppress during replay and initial loading. Legacy trophies preserve backfill provenance. Saved businesses, cash, inventory, and historical unlock records remain intact.

Verification: `node --test tests/achievements.test.js tests/store.test.js` checks reveal rules, prerequisites, layout, actual seeded runs, collection persistence and session isolation. A real rules career plus one continued chapter and normal outpost guidance earns all 78 trophies allowed by seed 42 by minute 82546; actual seeds 43 and 44 earn the remaining two seeded trophies. Browser tests in `tests/achievements.spec.js` cover mouse dragging (including over icons), click suppression, hover and pinned details, cursor-anchored wheel zoom, full-tree bounds, panel height, keyboard panning, branch shortcuts, desktop/mobile layout, collected trophies, banners, replay, new-business carryover, and reload.
